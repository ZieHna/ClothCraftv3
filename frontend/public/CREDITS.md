# Tab and home-screen icons

`favicon.ico` (16, 32 and 48 px), `favicon-32.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` and `apple-touch-icon.png` (180 px) are generated by
`frontend/scripts/make-icons.mjs` (`node scripts/make-icons.mjs`) from `assets-v1/logo.png`, the project logo (the file
`scripts/optimise-images.mjs` also reads for the header logo). The 16, 32 and 48 px icons use a tighter crop around the letters with a
little extra contrast and sharpening, so they stay readable in a tab; the larger ones are the full logo.

**Source and licence not confirmed.** The logo came from the v1 project's assets (`assets-v1/`). Who made it, and under what licence,
is not recorded anywhere in this repo and was not verified (the same open point as the garment photos in `public/garments/CREDITS.md`).
These icons are derivative works of it. Confirm the logo may be used publicly, or replace `assets-v1/logo.png` with one you own and run
the script again, before launch.

`site.webmanifest` only names the site and points at the 192 and 512 px icons; its colours are the site's `--color-paper` and
`--color-paper-warm` (a manifest cannot read CSS variables).
