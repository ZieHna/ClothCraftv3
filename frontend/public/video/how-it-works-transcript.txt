ClothCraft: how it works (15-second looping video, no speech)

01 CHOOSE. Pick your piece. Pick a garment, color and size.
02 DESIGN. Make it yours. Add text, an image or a shape.
03 ORDER. Place your order. Cash on delivery or bank transfer. Stamp: ORDER PLACED.
04 RECEIVE. Delivered to you. It arrives. You check it off. Stamp: DELIVERED.
